EU PPWR · (EU) 2025/40

Your product passes. Does its packaging?

100mg/kgLead, cadmium, mercury and chromium(VI), added together
  • The PPWR has applied since 12 August 2026 to every pack sold in the EU, wherever it was made.
  • Food-contact packs must stay within the PFAS limits. We screen with total organic fluorine (TOF).
  • Each pack needs a technical file and an EU declaration of conformity, export cartons included.

Bring a pack. We'll tell you which tests it needs.

  • Evidence kept behind every number
  • Reports aligned with GRI, IFRS S1 and S2, and CSRD

GHG · Net Zero · ISO 14064

Measure the baseline. Plot the path to Net Zero.

  • GHG inventories for Scope 1, 2 and 3, to the GHG Protocol and ISO 14064
  • Carbon footprints with hotspots and reduction guidance
  • Net Zero strategies aligned with SBTi, and carbon credit roadmaps
  • Higg FEM verification, LCA, and ESG reports aligned with GRI, CSRD and IFRS

Bring a year of energy and fuel records. We'll sketch your Scope 1 and 2 with you.

Environment · ZDHC · EPA

Air, water, soil and noise. One lab tests them all.

  • Stack emissions, ambient and indoor air, under Pakistan EPA regulations
  • Wastewater, effluent and sludge at a ZDHC approved wastewater lab
  • ZDHC MRSL V3.1 approved certifier (Level 1)
  • Soil, waste and industrial hygiene, plus EIA and EPA NOC support

Bring your last EPA or ZDHC report. We'll plan the next sampling round.
